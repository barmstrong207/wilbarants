THE ORIGINAL WILBA RANTS

The original series of Wilba Rants were published on YouTube between July 4th, 2014 and ended, with Wilba's passing, a year later, on July 4th 2015.

There are bits of wisdom hidden within the absurdity of these original Rants. From the opening lines, where he unveils his philosophy of "I Did It All On Purpose" to his prophesying of his own untimely death in the closing lines of "I Had It In My Mouth, But I Forgot To Drag It."

In these Rants you'll uncover his confusion regarding science, religion, the origins of holidays, and everything else. Laugh at his ignorance and use it to recognize your own so that you can laugh at yourself as well.

These Rants were never meant to be the full story, and you can see the incredibly raw art and animation style, that somehow works so well with Wilba's nonsense. Everyone involved in these Rants is incredibly proud of them and friends of Wilba hold onto them dearly.

Thank you for enjoying the company of our good friend and laughing with us all these years later.
